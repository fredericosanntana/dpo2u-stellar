## Notes for judges
- The public claim is the Stellar load-bearing integration: canonical registry decision → admission or block in the positive set lane.

- The DeFindex legal framing is a stack: LGPD for privacy/disclosure, PSAV/VASP for operator/safeguards, CVM 175 for institutional vault mandate/rebalance governance, and Travel Rule as one transfer/reporting circuit rather than the whole regime.
- The honest boundary is explicit: external audited instance is publicly readable, but not mutable without admin authorization.
- Production privacy pool / final governance are not claimed in this submission.
